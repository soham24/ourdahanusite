# Bordi or Dahanu Beach?

There are two beaches people mean when they talk about coming here, and they suit different trips. The short answer:

**Dahanu Beach** if you're staying in town and want easy access and a bit of life around you.

**Bordi** if you want quiet. Maharashtra Tourism describes it as clean, quiet and less crowded, and that's a fair summary — it has more of a village-and-orchard feel.

Bordi is around **17 km from Dahanu**, so it's a proper trip rather than a stroll.

## Is Bordi worth the extra distance?

**Yes — if peace is the point.** Bordi plus the chikoo orchards around Gholvad is a genuinely slower, prettier version of this coast, and if you're staying up that way you get it on your doorstep.

**No — if you're here for one night** and staying in Dahanu town. Dahanu Beach is right there, and the time you'd spend travelling is better spent not travelling.

## Swimming: be careful

We're not going to tell you either beach is "safe for swimming," because that isn't a promise anyone can honestly make. Tides, currents and seasonal conditions change.

Go in shallow, watch what local people are doing, and don't assume calm-looking water is calm.

**In monsoon, don't swim.** The sea is rough and conditions can restrict activities generally. Monsoon here is for the green landscape, not the water.

## Best time to go

**Early morning**, or **the 1–1.5 hours before sunset**, through sunset. Middle of the day is hot and the light is flat.

Sunset is good at both. Bordi if you want fewer people in it.

## How clean are they?

Honestly: **Bordi is generally the cleaner and quieter of the two.** Dahanu Beach sees more activity and can carry more litter on busy days.

Neither is pristine, and after a long weekend the busier stretches show it. If cleanliness is what matters most to you, go to Bordi, and go on a weekday.

## Crowds

- **Weekday:** very quiet. You may have long stretches largely to yourself.
- **Weekend:** noticeably busier — but this is nothing like Juhu or Alibaug. "Busy" here still means space to sit.

## What's actually available

This is where we'd rather under-promise:

**Food** — basic stalls and vendors may be around, particularly at weekends. **Carry water.** Don't plan on finding a proper meal on the sand.

**Horse and camel rides** — availability is inconsistent. They're sometimes around. Don't build a child's day around the promise of one.

**Water sports** — Maharashtra Tourism mentions jet skiing, boating and canoeing at Bordi, but this is seasonal and operator-dependent. Check locally when you arrive rather than assuming.

**Toilets and changing rooms** — not reliable enough to promise. Plan accordingly, especially with kids.

**Parking** — generally available around the beach approaches. Exact paid/free arrangements change. Weekends and festival dates get busy, so arrive earlier.

## Which one, then?

| | Dahanu Beach | Bordi |
|---|---|---|
| Getting there | Easy from town | ~17 km |
| Atmosphere | More activity | Village and orchards |
| Cleanliness | Busier, more litter | Generally cleaner |
| Crowds | Busier | Quieter |
| Best for | One-nighters, convenience | A slower, quieter trip |

Staying in town for a night? Dahanu Beach.

Here for the weekend and want it peaceful? Bordi, and stay up that way.

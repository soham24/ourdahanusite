# A Weekend in Dahanu: Where to Stay, What to Eat, What It Costs

Most Dahanu travel pages will tell you it's a peaceful coastal getaway and leave you no wiser about what a night actually costs. Here's the practical version.

## What a weekend really costs

Ranges rather than promises, because your accommodation choice swings the total more than anything else:

| | One night, comfortable |
|---|---|
| **Couple** | roughly **₹5,000 – ₹12,000** |
| **Family of four** | roughly **₹8,000 – ₹20,000+** |

**Going lower is genuinely possible.** Take the local train instead of driving, pick a basic stay, eat where locals eat, and a couple can do this well under the bottom of that range. The train fare from Mumbai is small change.

**Going higher is easy too** — premium beach resorts on a long weekend will take you past the top of it.

The variable is almost entirely where you sleep. Food here is not expensive, and getting around costs very little.

## Where to stay

Four different kinds of trip, really:

**Beach resorts** — convenience and sea views, easiest if the beach is the point of the trip.
- *Sea Crest Beach Resort*, Dahanu — right by the beach
- *Treat Beach Resort & Spa*, Gholvad — the more upmarket option, around 500 m from Bordi beach

**Homestays** — quieter, smaller, more personal. Bordi and Gholvad are where these cluster.
- *Villa Phiroze Baug*, Bordi
- *Appachi Wadi*, Bordi

**Farm and agro stays** — for orchard country and a slower trip.
- *Save Farm*, Gholvad
- *Vivant Agro Tourism*

**Budget hotels** in town — sensible if beach atmosphere isn't what you came for and you just need a base.

**On rates:** check live prices before booking. They move a lot between weekdays, weekends and season, and any figure printed here would mislead you.

**Booking ahead:** Saturdays, long weekends and festival dates need earlier booking — particularly Bordi during the February Chikoo Festival, and anywhere near Dahanu during the Mahalaxmi yatra.

**Travelling as a big family or group?** Farm stays and villas are usually easier to arrange than blocking out several hotel rooms.

## Where to eat

Dahanu's strengths are **fresh seafood**, Maharashtrian and Konkani cooking, chikoo in every form, and — as you move up toward Bordi and Udvada — a genuine **Parsi influence**.

**Seafood**
- *Hotel Darya Darshan Sea Food*
- *Pearline Restaurant & Bar* — beach-side, non-veg

**Local and general**
- *Hotel Sarovar*

**Cafés and vegetarian**
- *Rever Cafe*, Dahanu
- *Rever Pure Veg*

**What a seafood meal costs:** roughly **₹200–₹500 per person** for most fish, and meals in the ₹200–400 band are common. Premium fish can run well above that — **ask the rate before you order**, because it's priced by what came in that day, not by a fixed menu price.

**Jain food:** ask a vegetarian restaurant directly for Jain preparation rather than assuming it's on the menu.

**Parsi food:** you'll do better around Bordi and Udvada than in Dahanu town. If you're making the Udvada trip, plan to eat there — for a lot of visitors it's the main draw.

**Warli food** isn't something you'll find on a restaurant menu. It comes through farm stays and community experiences, if at all.

**One thing to plan around: Dahanu is not a late-night town.** Don't arrive at nine expecting Mumbai hours. Eat earlier than you would at home.

## What costs nothing

- Both beaches, and the sunset from either
- Walking the town
- The temple
- The orchard countryside — worth a drive on its own

Farm tours and Warli workshops usually charge separately, so budget for those if you want them.

## A sensible weekend

**Saturday** — early train or drive up, check in, lunch, afternoon on the beach, sunset at Bordi, seafood dinner.

**Sunday** — orchard visit or Mahalaxmi temple in the morning, unhurried lunch, leave by mid-afternoon to stay ahead of the Sunday-evening traffic into Mumbai.

That's the trip most people are actually looking for.

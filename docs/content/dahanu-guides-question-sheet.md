# Dahanu Guides — Question Sheet

**How to use this:** Answer in bullets, fragments, or voice notes. Don't write prose — that's my job. Half-answers are fine; "I don't know" is a useful answer too, it tells me to cut that section rather than invent it.

**Ground rule:** if you're not sure of a number (price, timing, distance), say "not sure" rather than guessing. A guessed train fare that's wrong is worse than no fare at all — it's the thing that makes a reader stop trusting the whole site.

**Order matters.** They're ranked by search demand × how easy they are for you to answer. Do #1 first; it alone is worth more than the last three combined.

⭐ = essential, the article doesn't work without it
○ = nice to have, adds depth

## 1. Getting to Dahanu from Mumbai ⭐ START HERE

The highest-traffic page you will ever publish. Everyone visiting searches this first.

**By train**
- ⭐ Which trains do you actually recommend, by name/number if you know them?
- ⭐ Roughly how long does the journey take?
- ⭐ Rough fare — second class vs first class vs AC?
- ⭐ Which Mumbai stations can you board from? (Churchgate / Bandra / Borivali / Vasai — which is best?)
- ⭐ **Dahanu Road vs Dahanu** — what's the difference, and which do visitors actually want? (This confuses everyone. Explaining it clearly is genuinely valuable.)
- Are there specific trains to avoid — too crowded, awkward timing, unreserved chaos?
- Is booking ahead necessary, or can you just turn up?
- ○ Where's the ladies' coach, roughly, on the platform?
- ○ First/last train of the day, if you know them?

**Arriving at the station**
- ⭐ You step off at Dahanu Road. What's actually there? Autos waiting? Do you need to book ahead?
- ⭐ Rough auto fare to the beach / to Bordi / into town?
- Do autos use meters, or is it fixed/negotiated? What should someone expect to pay so they don't get overcharged?
- ○ Anywhere at/near the station to eat or wait?

**By road**
- ⭐ Route from Mumbai — which highway, roughly how long?
- ⭐ Rough toll + fuel cost one way?
- Where do people usually stop to eat on the way?
- Any turn or junction people commonly get wrong?
- ○ Parking at the beaches — free? paid? does it fill up?

**Timing**
- ⭐ Best time of day to travel, and why?
- ⭐ What changes in monsoon? Roads, trains, anything to warn people about?
- Weekend vs weekday difference?
